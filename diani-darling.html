01•Arrival & Location

Prime Diani Beach Road Sanctuary

150m (3-Min Walk) to White Sands 24/7 Guarded Boom Barrier

Situated directly along premier Diani Beach Road, Beakim Diani Darling delivers absolute peace of mind. Arrive through a private manned security gate equipped with vehicle boom barriers, active perimeter electric fencing, and continuous night patrol. Africa’s top-rated white-sand shoreline is just 150 meters away down a gentle, shaded footpath.

02•Vehicle Convenience

Eco-Friendly Solar-Canopy Parking

15+ Personal Vehicles & Tour Vans High-Efficiency Solar Array

Drive in with complete convenience. Our expansive, heavy-duty cabro-paved parking bay is sheltered under a continuous high-capacity solar carport. It protects guest vehicles from the tropical midday sun while harvesting sustainable clean energy for the resort. With dedicated spaces for 15+ personal SUVs, safari land cruisers, and group tour coasters, your vehicle is guarded around the clock.
