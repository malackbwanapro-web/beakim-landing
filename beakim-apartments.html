How do long-term lease rates work?

Long-term rates depend on lease duration (e.g. 1 month, 6 months, 1 year). Please call or WhatsApp management directly to negotiate rates.

Is the swimming pool open to residents?

Yes, all residents in our 20 apartments have full complimentary access to the resort swimming pool and sun lounges.

For short-term nightly bookings, all utilities (electricity, AC, Wi-Fi, water, security) are fully included in the nightly rate.
