The Ultimate Diani Beach Guide: Arriving via the New Dongo Kundu Bypass Highway

For years, reaching Diani Beach from Nairobi required enduring the notorious Likoni Ferry queue in Mombasa island. Today, the game has completely changed. Thanks to the newly commissioned Dongo Kundu Bypass Highway, your trip from the Miritini SGR Terminus directly to Ukunda now takes just 45 to 60 minutes of uninterrupted driving.

⚡ Essential Route Takeaways

  • Zero Ferry Delays: The dual-carriageway bridge skirts entirely around Mombasa island, connecting directly to the Likoni-Lunga Lunga Highway.
  • Train Arrival Timing: Arrive on the morning Madaraka Express (10:30 AM arrival) and you will be checking in or lounging by our swimming pool before 12:00 PM.
  • Pre-Arranged SGR Pickups: Beakim Hospitality coordinates verified private local drivers to meet your carriage with a sign (KES 3,000 – KES 4,000 per private car).
  • Ukunda Airstrip (UKA): Prefer flying directly? Jambojet, Safarilink, and AirKenya touch down at Ukunda Airstrip, just 5–10 minutes from all 4 Beakim properties.
