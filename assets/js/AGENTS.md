# Beakim Landing - Client Scripts Domain (`assets/js/`)

## 1. Purpose & Ownership
- **Scope:** Public client-side JavaScript for interactive UI widgets, dynamic stay pricing computation, gallery lightboxes, WhatsApp inquiry formatting, and background sync to Beakim CRM.
- **Key Modules Owned:**
  - `main.js`: Main client orchestrator, booking form serialization, date pickers, luxury range calendar popover (`BeakimDateRangePicker` portaled to `document.body` on mobile to avoid ancestor `backdrop-blur` containing block bugs, decomposed into SRP sub-renderers `buildStatusGuidanceHtml`, `buildCalendarPresetsHtml`, `buildCalendarMonthNavHtml`, `buildCalendarDaysGridHtml`, `buildCalendarDayCellHtml` returning accessible `<button>` tags with full WCAG `aria-label` and `aria-pressed`), honeypot extraction, WhatsApp URL generation, review modal, pure `calculateReservationTotals` engine, pure date predicates (`isPastDate`, `isInvalidDateRange`, `isDateBetween`, `formatTwoDigitNumber`), non-blocking CRM boundary adapter (`CrmSyncService`), storage adapter (`SafeStorageService`), and communication gateway (`CommunicationService`).
  - `catalog.js`: Single Source of Truth for verified property offerings, nightly/monthly rates, conference packages, room amenities, `BEAKIM_POLICIES` (50% deposit, cancellation tiers, 8AM–8PM desk hours with 24/7 dispatch), and authoritative 76-unit portfolio inventory (Darad: 45, Darling: 16, Apartments: 12 [Under Renovation], Tower: 3 [2-Bedroom, KSh 2,500/night, 4 guests max, 0 pools]).
- **Boundaries & Invariants:**
  - **Dynamic Ancillary Pricing & Add-On Eligibility:** Breakfast rates are property-sensitive and resolved via `resolveBreakfastRate(propertyIdentifier)`: KSh 500 / person / day at Beakim Darad, KSh 1,000 / person / day at Diani Darling Cottages, and KSh 0 (self-catering / not offered) at Tower Cottages and Beakim Apartments. `syncBreakfastAddonCard(propertyValue)` targets `#addon-breakfast-price-badge` and `#addon-breakfast-description` to dynamically render `+KSh 1,000 / person / day` (verandah delivery) for Diani Darling or `+KSh 500 / person / day` (poolside dining) for Beakim Darad, and `handleDateOrAddonChange()` updates `#summary-breakfast-label` to display the active per-person daily rate in the live stay summary. When Tower Cottages is selected, it completely hides `#addon-card-breakfast`, unchecks and disables `#addon-breakfast`, hides the entire preferences step (`#booking-step-preferences`), and dynamically renumbers `#booking-step-guest-badge` to Step 2 for a frictionless 2-step journey (*1. Residence & Dates* → *2. Guest Information*). Switching back to Darad or Darling restores Step 2, re-enables breakfast options, updates pricing badges, and sets the guest step badge back to 3.
  - **Truthful Network & Intake Delivery:** `CrmSyncService.sendInquiry` executes asynchronously with an AbortController timeout (`APP_CONFIG.CRM.SYNC_TIMEOUT_MS: 4500`). Network delays, cold starts, or offline states must NEVER mislead the visitor with a false confirmation receipt: client preserves draft details in `localStorage` via `SafeStorageService`, renders `renderInquiryNetworkFailure` with an instant retry button, direct telephone access, and seamless pre-filled WhatsApp handoff.
  - **No Magic Numbers:** Centralized configurations are strictly frozen under `APP_CONFIG` (`CONTACT`, `SOCIAL`, `RATES`, `CRM`, `UI`, `TIME`, `DEFAULTS`, `CALENDAR`). Official social channels (Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube) are centrally configured in `APP_CONFIG.SOCIAL`. Calendar timing and labels are in `APP_CONFIG.CALENDAR`. Conference packages and direct desk reception lines are referenced strictly from `BEAKIM_CATALOG` in `catalog.js`.
  - **CommonJS Test Compatibility:** All pure functions and boundary services are exported conditionally at the bottom of `main.js` for zero-dependency Node.js unit tests (32 automated assertions in `test/clean_code.test.js`).
  - **Unit Capacity & Multi-Unit Booking Protocol:**
    - Single-unit capacities are strictly enforced in `catalog.js` and `validateBookingFormData`: All standard/superior rooms and ensuite/deluxe studios feature 1 bed (max 2 guests); Garden View Cottage features 1 King Bed (max 2 adults + 1 child, 3 total guests); Tower Cottages 2-Bedroom features 2 Queen beds (max 4 guests).
    - If selected party size exceeds single-unit capacity, `handleDateOrAddonChange()` and `triggerLiveAvailabilityCheck()` immediately show `#booking-units-wrapper` with dynamic guidance text and render `renderAvailabilityCapacityWarning(...)` instead of false availability pre-approval.
    - Capacity warning displays bed type, party size vs capacity, minimum units required (`Math.ceil(totalGuests / maxGuests)`), a 1-click button to select the required units (calling `setBookingUnitsCount(units)`), and cross-sells Tower Cottages 2-Bedroom for parties $\le 4$.
    - `calculateReservationTotals` scales `baseCost = singleUnitCost * unitsCount`, updating live price summaries and WhatsApp inquiry copy itemization (`(${unitsCount} Units)`).

## 2. Local Contracts & Rules
- **CRM Sync Protocol:** Submissions POST to `getCrmPublicEndpoint()`, resolving `http://localhost:3005/api/public/inquiries` in local development and `https://crm.beakimhospitalitygroup.co.ke/api/public/inquiries` in production. Supports automatic test/demo tagging via URL search parameter `?demo=true` or `?test=true` (payload `isTest: true`) with subtle floating status banner.
- **Frictionless Zero-Leak Booking Intake & Live Availability:**
  - `triggerLiveAvailabilityCheck()`: Real-time debounced date availability checking via `CrmSyncService.checkAvailability({ propertyId, checkInDate, checkOutDate })`, rendering dynamic status badges (`Available`, `High Demand / Limited`, `Sold Out`) in `#availability-status-container`.
  - Preferred Contact Channel selector (`WhatsApp / Text`, `Phone Call`, `Email`) captured in `extractBookingFormData()` and sent to CRM as `preferredContactMethod`.
  - 2-Stage Confirmation Modal: Stage 1 displays summary with `"✓ Confirm & Submit Reservation Inquiry"`. Stage 2 displays an on-site luxury receipt with verified Reference ID (`#INQ-...`), guaranteed 15–30 min response time SLA, and an *optional* WhatsApp helper button that never blocks submission or requires external apps. Both stages use a 3-zone viewport-safe flex container (`max-h-[90vh] flex flex-col`) with a pinned top header (and high-contrast circular `×` close button), scrollable middle body, pinned bottom action footer, and background page scroll lock managed by `PageScrollLock` with reference counting (`lock()`, `unlock()`, `reset()`).
  - **Intake Honesty (Zero False Confirmation):** On network failure, offline state, or server error, `confirmAndSubmitInquiry` NEVER displays a false confirmation receipt. Instead, it renders `renderInquiryNetworkFailure` providing transparent error messaging, an instant retry button (`Retry Reservation Submission`), direct call assistance, and pre-formatted WhatsApp direct fallback with client inquiry details intact (A204).
- **Anti-Spam:** Honeypot field (`website_hp`) must be submitted alongside form values.
- **2-Column Reservation Command Center & Progressive Disclosure:** Interactive residence cards (`.residence-card`), curated stay preferences (early SGR bag drop, self-catering kitchen setup, ground-floor preference), dynamic 5-tier room category sub-selector for Beakim Darad (`selectDaradRoomTier()`, `toggleDaradRoomSelector()`, `syncDaradRoomTiersStyling()`, `#darad-room-selector-container`), and authentic property-sensitive breakfast add-on (KSh 500 at Darad, KSh 1,000 at Darling) with real-time live price recalculation decomposed into SRP sub-handlers (`selectResidenceCard()`, `syncPropertyDropdown()`, `syncResidenceCardsStyling()`, `syncBreakfastAddonCard()`, `handleDateOrAddonChange()`). Deployed site-wide across `index.html`, `tower-cottages.html`, `diani-darling.html`, and `beakim-darad.html` with property-specific tailoring.
- **Reception Desk Direct Lines:** Beakim Beach Darad Reception: `+254 750 864 119` (`0750 864 119`); Diani Darling Reception: `+254 119 237 439` (`0119 237 439`); Central Hotline: `+254 746 938 529`.
- **Ergonomics & A11y:** Full keyboard navigation support for residence radio cards via `handleResidenceCardKeydown()` (`Enter` and `Space`), and async submission locking on `#booking-submit-btn` with `aria-busy="true"` and `active:scale-[0.98]` tactile press feedback.
