Guest Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers tailored for local Kenyan travelers, international visitors, and group organizers.

How do we get to Beakim properties from Miritini SGR station without using the Likoni Ferry?

+

You do not need to use the Likoni Ferry at all! Thanks to the newly completed Dongo Kundu Bypass highway, your taxi or vehicle travels directly from Miritini SGR Terminus to Ukunda and Diani Beach in 45 to 60 minutes of uninterrupted highway driving.

What are your payment methods and deposit requirements? How do we verify our booking?

+

We accept 100% transparent M-Pesa payments via our official business M-Pesa Buy Goods Till / Paybill Number under Beakim Hospitality Group. A 50% deposit locks your reservation dates, and the remaining 50% balance is payable upon check-in after inspecting your unit.

• You receive an instant SMS payment confirmation and official booking voucher via WhatsApp.

Is it safe to park our personal vehicle at your properties if we drive down from Nairobi?

+

Yes, 100% safe! All four Beakim properties are fully enclosed gated compounds featuring perimeter security walls, electric fencing, 24/7 manned security guards, and active CCTV camera coverage. On-site private parking is provided completely free of charge inside the compound for all resident guest vehicles.

Where do we buy food and groceries, and can we hire a private Swahili chef?

+

All our 1-Bedroom and 2-Bedroom units feature fully equipped kitchens (gas stove, fridge, cookware, microwave). Tower Cottages is right behind Naivas Supermarket Ukunda (30 seconds walk). Beakim Darad and Beakim Apartments are 4 minutes drive from Naivas, Chandarana Foodplus, and local produce markets.

• We connect you with local fishermen for fresh daily seafood delivery (red snapper, prawns, lobster) and can assign an experienced Swahili chef (KSh 1,500 – 2,500/day) to prepare authentic meals!

How far are the 5th-row properties from the beach and ocean swimming access points?

+

Our Beach Road properties (Beakim Darad & Diani Darling) are a 2–4 minute walk to the beach. Our 5th-row properties (Beakim Apartments & Tower Cottages) are just a 3 to 5-minute drive (~1.5–2.5 km) or a quick KSh 50–100 tuktuk ride to free public beach access points like Kongo Mosque Beach, Baharini, and Tradewinds.

How do airport transfers work from Ukunda Airstrip (UKA) and Mombasa Airport (MBA)?

+

• Ukunda Airstrip (UKA): Located in Diani, just 5–10 minutes from all Beakim properties. Taxis cost ~KES 1,200 ($10 USD) or tuktuks KES 300 ($2.50 USD).

• Mombasa Airport (MBA): The Dongo Kundu Bypass highway connects the airport directly to Diani in 45–60 minutes without ferry bottlenecks. Private taxi transfers cost KES 4,500 – 6,500 ($35 – $50 USD).

Is the Wi-Fi fast and reliable for remote work, video calls, and digital nomads?

+

Yes! We welcome digital nomads and remote workers. All Beakim properties feature free high-speed fiber optic Wi-Fi (20 Mbps – 50 Mbps upload/download) capable of handling HD Zoom calls, streaming, and software work. Work desks, outdoor patio seating with power outlets, and power backup systems are provided.

How do credit cards, ATMs, and M-Pesa work for foreign tourists in Kenya?

+

Kenya is a global leader in mobile money. Upon arrival, purchase a local Safaricom SIM or eSIM with your passport. You can load cash onto M-Pesa or top up directly from foreign debit/credit cards using apps like Wise or Remitly. Major supermarkets (Naivas) and ATMs (KCB, NCBA, Absa) accept Visa and Mastercard.

Can I drink tap water in Diani, and what medical facilities are nearby?

+

Do not drink tap water in Diani. Tap water is non-potable and intended for showering and washing. Use bottled mineral water for drinking and brushing teeth (available at Naivas for <$1 USD per 5L jug). For medical peace of mind, Diani Beach Hospital is a top international-standard private hospital with a 24/7 ER on Beach Road.

How do I handle local beach operators ("beach boys") politely and safely?

+

Local beach operators selling tours or wood carvings are friendly and harmless, but persistent. The best response is a warm smile paired with Swahili phrases: "Asante, hapana" (Thank you, no) or "Niko sawa" (I'm all set). Once communicated firmly and respectfully, they will politely wish you a pleasant day.

What is your group capacity, and can we book an entire apartment block exclusively?

+

We accommodate groups from 10 up to 150+ guests across our hospitality portfolio. At Beakim Darad (45 units with 2 resort pools) and Tower Cottages, groups can reserve connected room clusters or cottage blocks—giving your school, church, or corporate team private sections, lawn access, and dedicated compound coordination without interference from outside guests.

What catering and meal options are available for groups of 20 to 100+ people?

+

We offer 3 flexible catering models:

1. Self-Catering: Prepare meals in fully equipped unit kitchens (Naivas supermarket next door for bulk groceries).

2. Resident Chef Services: Hire resident chefs for labor-only prep (saving 40-50% vs hotel buffets) or full-board catering.

3. Poolside Dining & Nyama Choma: Set up long buffet lines and evening Nyama Choma (BBQ) events on poolside lawns at Beakim Darad or Diani Darling Cottages.

Is there bus/coaster parking, and do you issue official KRA ETR tax invoices & accept LPOs?

+

Yes to all! Our compounds feature wide entry gates and secure parking for 25-seater coasters and 51-seater buses. We issue formal Pro-Forma Invoices, official KRA ETR Tax Invoices for corporate VAT compliance, and accept verified corporate/school LPOs and bank wire transfers.

Can our group conduct site visits prior to booking?

+

We actively encourage pre-booking site inspections! Group organizers, HR committees, and trip leaders are invited to inspect rooms, kitchens, pool areas, and security setups prior to confirming reservations.

What are your standard check-in and check-out times?

+

Standard check-in is from 2:00 PM onwards, and check-out is by 10:00 AM. If you arrive early via SGR train or morning flight, we offer complimentary secure luggage holding and access to poolside changing facilities so you can start relaxing immediately.

What is included in the nightly price? Are utilities charged extra?

+

Our nightly rates include the listed accommodation services and utilities: complete accommodation, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, electricity/water utilities, daily housekeeping, 24/7 gated security, and free on-site parking. Air conditioning and swimming pool access are included according to property and category specifications (e.g., split AC in cottages, superior rooms, and deluxe studios; cooling ceiling fans in standard rooms; swimming pools at Darad and Darling, with zero pools at Tower Cottages). Optional breakfast is priced separately: KSh 500 per person/day at Darad and KSh 1,000 at Diani Darling.
