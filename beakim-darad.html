Frequently Asked Questions
Are both swimming pools accessible to all guests?

Yes! Guests staying in Standard Rooms, Deluxe Studio Rooms, or 1-Bedroom Units enjoy full complimentary access to both resort swimming pools.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in is from 12:00 PM onwards, and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged upon request subject to availability.

Is there secure parking available?

Yes, we provide free private parking inside a 24/7 guarded and gated compound for all resident guests.
